The Nike TN, or Air Max Plus, is a sneaker known for its "Tuned Air" (TN) technology, which uses a system of plastic columns and air cushions for personalized cushioning and stability. Originally designed as a running shoe, it features a distinctive aesthetic with wavy lines inspired by palm trees and a whale's tail arch shank. The shoe became iconic in youth culture, especially in Europe and Australia, due to its bold, eye-catching design. .
